## Notes

- Downloads were made with a plain HTTP client, from URLs linked on the public site or listed in its public sitemap. `robots.txt` disallows only `/wp-admin/` and `/wp-content/uploads/wpforms/`. None of the downloaded paths are disallowed.
- Images are served through `next/image`, which converts them to AVIF or WebP on request. Sources are not modified.
- The PDFs are served as published. Each PDF link opens in a new tab with a visible note.
