## 2. The review engine

**What it is for.** The map pack ranks on relevance, distance and prominence, and prominence is mostly the number, recency and rating of Google reviews. Nearby winners showed 32 to 173 reviews. Apollo's 5 Nov reading was 4.4 stars from 101 Google reviews (recheck the live number), so the goal is a steady flow and good replies, not a rescue.

**Rules, written into the routine (these come from Google's review policy and the FTC's rule on fake and incentivised reviews, 16 CFR 465):**
1. Ask every patient, not only happy ones. Never screen who gets asked.
2. Never offer anything in return: no gift, discount, entry, or "we'll move you up".
3. Never write or edit a review for a patient, and never ask staff or family to post reviews.
4. Never ask for a particular rating or particular words.
5. Reply to every review within two working days. Never confirm that someone is a patient, and never put any health detail in a reply.
6. Never ask for reviews on Yelp: Yelp forbids it. Claim and correct the page only.

**Get the link.** In the Google Business Profile, open "Get more reviews" (or "Ask for reviews") and copy the short link and the QR code. Use that same link in all materials below. Healthgrades and Zocdoc send their own surveys; do not copy them.

**Front-desk script (after checkout):** "Thank you for coming in. If you have a minute, a review on Google helps other families find a doctor. This card has a code you can scan. Whatever you write is up to you."

**QR card text (business card size):**
> Apollo Physicians, 8191 Timberlake Way, Suite 200, Sacramento. Tell other families about your visit: scan to leave a Google review. Your honest opinion is what matters. Thank you. 916-236-5800

**Text-message template** (only to patients who agreed to text messages; ask your compliance adviser first, because texting patients falls under privacy and messaging rules):
> Thank you for visiting Apollo Physicians. If you would like to share your experience, you can leave a review here: [Google link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

The message must name no visit reason, diagnosis or provider detail beyond the practice name, and must go through a messaging tool that fits the practice's privacy obligations.

**Reply templates** are in `offsite-2026-11-12.md` section 4 (positive, negative about service, mentions clinical care, wrong place or suite).

## 5. Awards: what can and cannot be done

- **Sacramento Magazine Top Docs** (sacramentotopdocs.com) is a peer vote run by an outside research firm. Physicians vote; patients cannot. Write-ins are allowed. The cost of a listing is not stated (unverified). **Castle Connolly** is peer nomination plus vetting; a physician cannot pay to be selected. **ThreeBestRated** says its listings are not for sale. None can be bought or self-nominated, so Apollo cannot make any of them happen.
- If Dr. Panesar is ever included, publish **one dated, factual, attributed line with a link**, for example: "Listed in Sacramento Magazine Top Docs, 2027 (source: [link])." Never a badge or phrase that implies a current ranking, never "best", and never a paid "Top Doctor" badge or sponsored list.
- The site's `claims-guard.test.ts` currently bans "award" and "ranked" everywhere, so adding such a line needs a deliberate test change in the same commit, made only after the award is real.

## 6. Do not

Do not buy reviews, review packages or "reputation boosting". Do not use fake or duplicate profiles. Do not list Apollo under a community-clinic or FQHC category. Do not add the Suite 400 doctor's details to any Apollo listing. Do not publish star ratings or review counts on the website.
